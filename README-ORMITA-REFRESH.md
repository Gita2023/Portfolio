## What was improved
- Rewrote project descriptions to follow a clearer business narrative: context, analysis, findings and recommended actions.
- Improved Arabic phrasing and aligned it with the English descriptions.

- Updated page titles and the homepage brand text.

## Accuracy notes
- Numeric findings are retained from the supplied portfolio pages and have not been independently audited.
- Descriptions explain what the supplied project content supports; they do not claim additional tools, data sources or business outcomes.
- Some original video embeds in the annotation page use placeholder YouTube IDs. They have been retained rather than replaced with invented links.
- Please review the figures and examples before publishing, especially if the underlying datasets or dashboard versions have changed.
