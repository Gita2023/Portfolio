Named Entity Recognition (NER)

Purpose:
Identifies specific entities in unstructured text and assigns each one a consistent label, such as PERSON, ORGANIZATION or LOCATION. In the example, Label Studio is used to mark “Sarah Johnson,” “Microsoft” and “Seattle.” This structured output supports information extraction, knowledge graphs and question-answering applications.
